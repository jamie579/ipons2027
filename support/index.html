Support the conference

Help make space for thoughtful discussion about nursing, midwifery and the future of care.

28–30 July 2027 · Charité, Berlin, and online

The 29th International Nursing Philosophy Conference brings together nurses, midwives, researchers, educators and students. Together, we will explore the ideas shaping clinical practice, education, working lives and emerging technologies.

What support could make possible

We welcome conversations about support for participation and the exchange of ideas at IPONS 2027. Possibilities include helping with attendance costs or supporting an independently judged paper prize. The purpose and arrangements for each contribution would be agreed before support is accepted.

Acknowledgement

We can discuss acknowledgement on the conference website and in conference materials, with the wording and format agreed in advance.

Academic independence

The scientific and organising committee retains responsibility for the programme, selection of contributions and any prize judging. Support does not confer a role in those decisions.
