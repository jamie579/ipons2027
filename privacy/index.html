3. This website

3.1 Visiting the site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company, USA). When you open a page, GitHub’s servers receive your IP address and the technical details your browser sends, and keep them in server logs under GitHub’s own terms. We do not see these logs.

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads its fonts from its own server, not from a third party.

Because GitHub is based in the USA, your IP address is transferred there when you open a page. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest in running and securing the website.

3.2 Committee preview

Some pages are password protected for the organising committee. If you tick “Remember me”, your browser stores a hashed form of the password in its local storage, on your device only. We do not receive it. You can remove it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

3.3 Writing to us

If you write to contact@ipons2027.com, we use your email address and your message to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) if we are in contact about you taking part. We delete the correspondence when the matter is closed, unless it concerns a submission, in which case section 4.5 applies.

The forwarding of this address is provided by our domain registrar, Namecheap, Inc.

4. Submitting an abstract or a panel

Submissions are collected with Microsoft Forms in Charité’s Microsoft 365.

4.1 What we collect

  • Your name, email address, institution and country.
  • For an individual abstract: the title, the abstract and optional co-authors.
  • For a panel: the panel title and abstract, and for each speaker a name, institution and email address, with a talk title and the name of the chair if you give them.
  • How you plan to attend (in Berlin or online), and for a panel whether any speaker will join online.
  • Your answer to the consent question.
  • The date and time of your submission.

We do not ask for health information, or whether you have a doctorate, in this form. If we need something like that later, for example for registration, accessibility arrangements or reports to funders, we will ask then and give you a separate notice.

4.2 Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • Reviewing your submission, selecting contributions and contacting you. Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, your consent, given by ticking the box in the form.
  • Publishing the title and abstract of an accepted contribution in the programme. If your contribution is accepted we will ask you separately, at that point, whether we may publish its title and abstract. We publish nothing without your answer, and saying no does not affect your place. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

We do not make decisions about you by solely automated means.

4.3 Panel speakers

If you submit a panel, you give us the details of your speakers. Please tell each speaker before you submit, and share this notice with them. We will contact speakers at the address you give. When we first write to a speaker we will point them to this notice.

4.4 Who receives your data

  • The organising committee.
  • Reviewers appointed by the committee, who see only what they need in order to review.
  • Microsoft, as the provider of Microsoft 365 under Charité’s agreement.
  • If your contribution is accepted, the public programme shows what you agreed to publish.

We do not sell your data. Responses are not shared by any public link.

4.5 How long we keep it

  • Declined and withdrawn submissions: until 31 October 2027, three months after the conference, then deleted.
  • Accepted submissions: until 31 December 2027. A programme you agreed to be published stays online as published.
  • Anything we must keep longer by law.

4.6 Do you have to provide it?

Name, email, institution and country, the submission itself and the consent answer are needed to consider your submission. Without them we cannot consider it. Co-authors, talk titles and the chair of a panel are optional.

5. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data (Art. 15 GDPR), correction (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and a copy in a common format where it applies (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(e) or (f) (Art. 21). You can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)); this does not affect what was done before.

To use these rights, write to the data protection officer (section 2) or to contact@ipons2027.com.

You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. For Charité this is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, telephone +49 30 13889-0, mailbox@datenschutz-berlin.de.
