What bodies can do

Berlin, July 2027

The 29th International Nursing Philosophy Conference, at Charité, Berlin.

IPONS 2027 brings together nurses, midwives, researchers, educators and students to explore nursing philosophy, ethics and the ideas shaping care. Taking place at Charité, Berlin, and online, the conference connects philosophical inquiry with clinical practice, education, working lives and emerging technologies.

In association with the International Philosophy of Nursing Society (IPONS).

About the conference

Our theme asks how care is shaped by our bodies, relationships, technologies and surroundings. Discussions will connect the everyday work of nurses and midwives with questions about knowledge, autonomy and the conditions of care.
