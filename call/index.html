Call for abstracts

What can a body do?

Submit your work

A body can heal, tire, comfort, resist, camp it up and carry on. Nobody has finished working out what else it can do. Nurses see a lot of it, and so do artists, engineers and activists. Send us what you have found, in nursing and care of every kind, science and technology studies, and beyond.
